Load the Kombi with friends and drive 100 km of dirt road, measured in real metres, through five biomes. One person drives; the others ride inside and watch the world go by through the windows.

The Kombi · Blender 5.2

A camper van built by code.

An original four-seat camper, modelled with Python scripts in Blender: they start from an empty scene and build the shell, the furniture and every small object inside. The colours borrow Firewatch's warm mood: terracotta, cream, petrol green and wood.

Modelled parts

Close enough to touch.

Blender → Unity

Four seats, four cameras.

Exported in metres with every moving part separate. In Unity the kit adds a Rigidbody, compound colliders and four WheelColliders, an outside camera for the driver and a first-person camera for each passenger, who can look 105° to either side and 65° up or down.

In the game

100 km, one stop at a time.

The world is generated by code from a seed and streamed around the player in 512 m tiles. The Kombi burns fuel and wears its tyres, so the trip turns into a string of stops: spot an abandoned cabin, park, cross the grass on foot and come back loaded. Cabins only show up on the map when someone runs the radar on the Kombi's roof. In between, fish, light a campfire and keep the wolves away.

100 kmof road, one unit to one metre
5biomes: valley, pine forest, plateau, autumn and highlands
32lakes and 2 rivers in one seed
6letters in a room code: online co-op through Unity Relay

Credits & AI The terrain, trees, grass and water are generated by code. Characters and their animations come from Mixamo, and the wolf is a free rigged model. The poster inside the van was made with an image model; the rest of the Kombi is geometry built by Python scripts. The turntable, exploded view, wireframe and single parts were rendered from the real .blend file in October 2026 for this page.

02 Co-op card game · Browser · Alpha

A Era dos Heróis

“The Age of Heroes”

A co-operative card game for up to four players that runs in the browser, with nothing to install. The heroes all act at the same time, against a dungeon that follows its own rules and hits back on its own.

Play the alpha ↗ Sign-in required

The party

No hero does everything.

Each hero has its own deck, an Ultimate, a mechanic nobody else has, and often a resource only they build up. The party covers the gaps.

Guardian · Tank Sir. Donovan de Valmont Resource Battle Charge

At the table

You against the dungeon.

  • Threat Every point of Energy the party burns raises the room's threat level.
  • Traps A new trap takes over each round and changes the rules for everyone.
  • Area attacks Monsters hit every living hero at once. Taunt, Shield and healing decide who stays up.

04 3D creature game · Unity · Android tablets · v0.8.0, in development

Mimo Garden

A 3D game where children create little dumpling creatures and look after them: mix a recipe, watch it steam, meet whoever is born, then bathe, feed and play.

  1. Mix Pour a colour and stir with any finger movement.
  2. Steam The lid drops, steam rises.
  3. Reveal “Look who was born!”
  4. Bathe Foam from 3,072 bubbles, rinsed off with a shower.
  5. Play Up to five friends share the park.

Materials

Made to be squeezed.

Pearl, jelly, soft and glitter-gel finishes. In surprise mode the recipe nudges the odds: more milk leans toward pearl, jelly and glitter gel.

05 Mobile game · in development

Dumpling Squish

It started with one question: does it make you want to squeeze it? Before any gameplay, a character study: a dumpling you can press, drag and spin, with a spring that makes it wobble back into shape.

  • 36,000 triangles in the final body, checked for open and non-manifold edges
  • 150-frame animation at 30 fps, made in Blender
  • Real time squash and stretch in the browser with Three.js

AI The body's base shape was generated in Tripo Studio. Face, materials, shape keys and animation were done in Blender.

How I build

AI writes the code with me. The game is my call.

I build with AI coding tools: Codex, Claude, Cursor and Gemini. I write the design and the specifications, direct the build and test every release. Where art in a project is AI-generated, the project says so, and so does this page.

  1. 01

    Design

    Rules, loops and feel, written down before any code: what the player does and what they should feel while doing it.

  2. 02

    Specify

    Every change becomes a written spec, along with the checks it has to pass.

  3. 03

    Direct

    AI tools build to the spec. I play the result and send it back until it is right.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Automated checks and real screen captures on each version, plus notes on what was not tested yet.
